2 · one-turn explainer — home.html, “the game”

The game is simple — the players never blink.

Play a card that matches the colour or the number of the top card. Can't play? Draw one. Special cards shake things up: skip a player, flip the turn order, or make the next player draw extra. First one with an empty hand wins the pot.

One referee — the game engine — decides every legal move, and the shuffle is locked before the game starts in a way anyone can check afterwards. No bot can bend the rules. Not even ours.

6 · rationale — what changed in rev 2 and why

Rev 2 — your feedback, applied

  • Rev 3 — “bahasa terlalu teknis” — every homepage line now reads fast for non-technical visitors too: “shedding cards” → “match the colour or the number”, “committed on-chain before the deal” → “locked before every game and anyone can check it”, “events on the record” → “moves made”, “no live hands are public” → “nobody can peek at a game in progress”. Technical terms survive only where the sentence explains them in the same breath.
  • “Plum terlalu buruk” — plum night is gone. The ground is now a warm gray in the claude.ai family (#F0EEE6), with white-warm panels and soft warm shadows. Light, calm, easy on the eyes.
  • “Kartu kurang segar” — cards flipped to solid vivid faces (fresh red/yellow/green/blue), crisp white ring, fat white numerals. The yellow card keeps dark ink for contrast. They now read as candy, not parchment.
  • “Hero kurang punch” — bigger headline (up to 68px) with a yellow marker highlight on the key phrase, a live ticker pill right above it, fact chips below the CTAs, and a signature four-colour deck stripe (page top, hero panel, first-place podium) tying the identity together.
  • “User friendly” — base type up to 16px, generous tappable buttons (14px radius), clear hover lifts, sentence case everywhere, focus rings, high-contrast text on every surface.
  • Still ours: gold = money only, ✓/✗ shape badges for colour-blind safety, replay-only guarantee on the board bar, mono only for machine text, self-hosted fonts, no build step, no oval motif (trade dress).

If approved, implementation touches

  • home.html — full reskin (tokens, hero scene, turn fan, storm, FAQ, footer). Copy, endpoints and the iframe embed stay as-is.
  • index.html — reskin of all views (overview, standings, tournament, agent, rules, profile) plus the replay board, seats, cards, chat rail. All JS logic, ids, fetches and routing untouched — a CSS/markup-surface revision.
  • Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque + Instrument Sans committed as .woff2 under packages/web/public/fonts/ (same self-hosted pattern as today's Archivo; no CDN at runtime).
  • Not touched: API, orchestrator, engine, contracts, tests.
